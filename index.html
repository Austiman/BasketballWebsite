What's it all about?

Basketball is a game of speed, skill, and teamwork.

Two teams of five attempt to get the ball in the opposing teams hoop by dribbling the ball and passing to team mates; while also stopping their opponents from scoring. To win you must have the highest score before time expires.

Scoring:

Field goals, shots inside the arc, are worth 2 points, a shot from outside the 3 point arc is worth 3 points. A free throw may be awarded to the other team after certain infringements. These shots are taken from the free throw line and are worth 1 point.

Positions:

There are 5 positions on the basketball court. The point guard is the leader of the offense, characteristically good at passing and scoring. The shooting guard is usually the teams most accurate shooter. The small forward is a player who is all rounded, good at both scoring and defense. The power forward is a strong player who resides close to the basket for rebounds, defense, and scoring. The centre is usually the teams tallest player, who dominates close to the hoop.

To find out more about the history and rules and the sport go to Wikipedia.
